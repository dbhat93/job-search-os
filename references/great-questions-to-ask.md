# Great Questions To Ask (Startup / Founder rounds)

A curated bank of high-signal questions to ask interviewers, especially useful in startup rounds and when talking to founders directly. Source: Jonathan Awad (CEO, Baselayer), shared 2026-09. The `questions` command should draw on this bank when the stage is a founder or startup round, adapting to what the candidate needs to learn.

## Use when situationally appropriate (great for startups / founders)

- Pick one person you've hired in the last year and tell me why they were an amazing hire.
- Tell me about someone in this role (or in general) who didn't work out. What happened?
- What are your pet peeves? What do you wish people did less of on the team?
- On a scale of 1 to 4, how sophisticated is the system already built for this role?
- Do you hope the person you hire in this role grows as an IC, or eventually becomes a manager?
- How long of a ramp period do you think someone needs in this role?
- How connected are Eng, Product, and Design with Sales and Marketing? What is the medium for connecting them?
- When did you think you hit PMF? Or if you haven't yet, how are you figuring out what PMF looks like for this business?
- When you look across your customers, what's the split in how you found them (inbound, outbound, referral, marketing-sourced)?
- What's the ideal team size for this team vs. where you are today?
- Do people work more in Slack, in meetings, or async in writing? How do people work best on the team?
- What would I need to do in this role to earn a raise every six months? How could I be invaluable to you and the team? Specifics, please.
- Are there upcoming launches or bets that could substantially change the company's trajectory?

## Avoid: questions founders do NOT enjoy (generic, low-signal)

These do not spark interesting conversation. Do not default to them.

- What inspired you to start the company?
- Now that you've raised more money, what's the plan to use it?
- What's the plan to grow this year?
- What are you looking for in the role?

## How to deploy

- Pick 2 to 4, not all of them. Match to what the candidate actually needs to evaluate the role and to what will make them memorable.
- The "someone who didn't work out" and "pet peeves" questions surface real signal about the team and the bar; use them with a founder or hiring manager, not a recruiter screen.
- The "raise every six months / be invaluable" question is strong with a founder because it signals ownership and ambition; deliver it plainly, not as a gotcha.
- The PMF and customer-split questions are best in exec/founder rounds and double as diligence on whether the company is worth joining.
